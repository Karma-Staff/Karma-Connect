KARMA CONNECT DIALER - WHAT THIS APP DOES ON YOUR COMPUTER

Karma Connect Dialer is Karma Staff's softphone for RingCentral calling. It
shows the Karma Connect dialer in a small window that stays on top of your
other apps and becomes a floating bubble when you minimise it.

Please read before installing:

1. MICROPHONE. The dialer uses your microphone and speakers for calls. Access
   is given only to Karma Connect (karma-connect-9s2z.onrender.com), and only
   for audio. The camera is never used.

2. STARTS WITH WINDOWS. The dialer opens when you sign in to Windows so it is
   ready for calls. To turn this off, right-click the bubble and untick
   "Start with Windows", or use Task Manager > Startup apps.

3. WHAT IS STORED ON THIS COMPUTER. Your Karma Connect sign-in (encrypted by
   Windows), the dialer's normal web cache, and where you last left the window
   and the bubble. Uninstalling removes all of it.

4. CONNECTIONS. The app talks to Karma Connect, to RingCentral for calls, and to
   GitHub to check for updates. It sends no analytics or tracking data.

5. UPDATES. New versions download in the background and install the next time
   you quit the dialer or restart your computer. A call is never interrupted.

Questions: contact your Karma Connect administrator.
